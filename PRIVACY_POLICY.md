# ClaudGPT — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 23, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes, at a general level, how information may be handled when using ClaudGPT, an AI-powered Telegram bot. This draft must be checked against the bot's actual data practices and its providers before being treated as a definitive description.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to information submitted to or generated through interactions with ClaudGPT on Telegram, including messages, files, images, audio, and requests to use bot features.

## 2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the features used and the bot's actual configuration, information may include:
- Telegram identifiers and information made available to the bot through Telegram.
- Messages, prompts, and conversation context submitted to the assistant.
- Images, documents, audio, project files, or other content submitted for processing.
- Content or results generated in response to user requests.
- Technical or operational information needed to operate, maintain, protect, or troubleshoot the service.

**The project must verify which of these categories it actually receives, records, or retains before publication.**

## 3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be processed as necessary to:
- Provide requested AI features and bot functionality.
- Process user-submitted messages, media, and files.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the service.
- Investigate misuse, operational issues, or security incidents.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Third-Party Processing

Some features may require sending user-submitted content to external AI, hosting, processing, or other service providers. Such providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

The project should identify relevant providers and verify what information is transmitted, the providers' retention practices, and any applicable data-use settings. See [THIRD_PARTY_SERVICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_SERVICES.md).

## 5. Storage and Retention

Information may be handled or retained by Telegram, ClaudGPT's systems, or third-party providers, depending on the feature and system configuration.

**This document does not establish specific retention periods or guarantee deletion.** The project must verify storage locations, retention periods, backups, logs, and deletion procedures before making specific commitments.

## 6. Sharing

Information may be transmitted to providers where needed to deliver a requested feature, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. The actual recipients and circumstances must be verified and described accurately.

The project does not make a blanket claim here that no data is shared or that data is never retained.

## 7. User Considerations

- Avoid sending passwords, payment details, highly sensitive information, or information you are not authorized to share.
- Consider that content submitted for AI processing may be handled by external providers.
- Review generated content before relying on it.

## 8. Privacy Requests and Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the project through its official communication channels. The project will review requests subject to technical limitations and applicable requirements.

## 9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the service or its data practices change. The latest version will be published in this repository.
