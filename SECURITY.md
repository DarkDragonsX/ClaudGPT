# ClaudGPT — Security Policy

## Reporting a Security Issue

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability affecting ClaudGPT, please report it through the project's official communication channels.

Please include, where possible:
- A clear description of the suspected issue.
- The affected feature or component.
- Steps to reproduce the issue, using only systems and data you are authorized to test.
- The potential impact.
- Any relevant screenshots, logs, or supporting details, with sensitive information removed.

## Responsible Disclosure

- Do not access, modify, download, or disclose other people's data.
- Do not disrupt service availability or perform destructive testing.
- Do not publicly disclose a suspected vulnerability before giving the project a reasonable opportunity to assess and address it.
- Stop testing if you encounter data that does not belong to you.

## Response

Reports will be reviewed as circumstances permit. This document does not promise a particular response time, reward, or outcome.

## Scope

This policy concerns security issues affecting ClaudGPT and its systems. Third-party services are outside the project's direct control; issues affecting those services should be reported to their respective owners.
